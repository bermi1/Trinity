BERMI TECHS × TRINITY MARK03 · Leadership
MODULES IN DETAIL

Seeing the whole company at a glance

01Command dashboard

The first screen management sees each morning: the live state of the company in one view, with every figure clickable down to the detail.

  • Active projects, today's revenue and expenses, pending approvals, outstanding invoices
  • Revenue vs expenses chart for 12 weeks, 6 months or the year
  • Live activity feed of approvals, stock movements and completed tasks
  • Quick actions: new request, task, lead, proposal or meeting
USED BY · Management
02Monday Briefing

Runs the weekly management meeting. Last week's results are prepared automatically; staff add their own presentations and reports before the meeting.

  • Previous-week summary: projects done, revenue, expenses, delays, carried-over tasks
  • Major issues flagged automatically — overdue invoices, delays, unreconciled floats
  • Staff upload presentations and department reports into the agenda
  • Decisions and actions become tasks; minutes are published to everyone
USED BY · Management · all staff
03Reports

Ready-made reports generated from live data — no more compiling spreadsheets by hand.

  • Weekly management report, project profit & loss, receivables
  • Money requests and floats, store stock, staff daily reports, production
  • Export to CSV for Excel, or PDF to share by email
USED BY · Management · finance · store

MODULES IN DETAIL

Running every project from brief to delivery

04Projects

Every client job becomes a project with its own budget, team, timeline, tasks, requests and documents — so profit is known during the job, not after it.

  • Contract value, budget, spend and progress side by side
  • Status flags: on track, at risk, late, completed
  • Project page links every task, money request, purchase and file
USED BY · Management · project managers · staff
05Tasks

Assigns and tracks work across the team so nothing depends on memory or WhatsApp.

  • Board view: to do, in progress, review, done
  • Owner, deadline and project on every card; overdue work highlighted
  • Staff see "My tasks"; managers see the whole team
USED BY · All staff
06Production

Follows every print and fabrication job through seven stages so the workshop, client and management see the same status.

  • Brief → design → client approval → production → quality check → packaging → delivery
  • Materials, quantity, cost and wastage recorded per job
  • Deadlines and responsible person on every job
